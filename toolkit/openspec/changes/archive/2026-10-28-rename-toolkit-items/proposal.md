## Why

The toolkit's items are named with no sign of what they are for, and more skills are coming for
design, communication and writing. A bare domain prefix such as `code-` would also collide with
built-in or third-party items (`code-review` is already a built-in skill). One naming pattern that
carries the owner and the domain keeps the toolkit readable as it grows and keeps its names its own.

## What Changes

- Name every item `jois-<domain>-<thing>`: the owner `jois` guards against collisions, the domain
  says what the item is for. Domains today: `git` and `code`.
- Skill: `coding-standards` becomes `jois-code-standards`.
- Rules, flat files as before: `core-principles`, `architecture-boundaries`, `java`, `python`,
  `typescript-react`, `config-files` and `scripts` become `jois-code-<name>.md`; `commit-hygiene`
  becomes `jois-git-commit-hygiene.md`.
- Commands: the `jois` namespace folder becomes `jois-git`, so `/jois:stage`, `/jois:write-pr` and
  `/jois:update-pr` become `/jois-git:stage`, `/jois-git:write-pr` and `/jois-git:update-pr`.
- `opsx` commands are unchanged: they are generated by the `openspec` CLI and not owned here.
- The installer, `check_toolkit.py`, the tests, the README and the specs follow the new names. The
  README states the naming pattern and the domain list.
- **BREAKING**: installed copies under the old names are no longer managed by the installer and
  load alongside the new ones until removed. The README gets a migration note (see design.md).

Rule contents, scoping (`paths:`) and `metadata:` versions are not changed by this rename.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None. The naming pattern is recorded in the existing `toolkit-skills` capability.

### Modified Capabilities

- `toolkit-skills`: items follow the `jois-<domain>-<thing>` naming pattern; the skill, command
  and installer requirements use the new names.
- `toolkit-coding-standards`: the skill and the rules are named with the `jois-code-` prefix and
  `commit-hygiene` with `jois-git-`; requirements that cite the old names are updated.
- `jois-stage`: the command is `/jois-git:stage`.

## Impact

- `toolkit/skills/coding-standards/` (folder and `name:`), `toolkit/rules/*.md` (8 files),
  `toolkit/commands/jois/` (folder and the commands' `name:` and cross-references).
- `toolkit/install/Install-Toolkit.ps1` (paths and examples naming the old items) and
  `toolkit/check_toolkit.py` plus `toolkit/tests/test_check_toolkit.py` (hardcoded paths).
- `toolkit/README.md`, the three specs above, and references between rules and skills.
- Machines with the toolkit already installed: the old items stay until removed by hand or with
  the old installer's `-Uninstall`.
